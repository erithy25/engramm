    About ENGRAMM

    ENGRAMM is a chat assistant that works without a neural network: counting, rules and hypervectors.

    • Facts come with their source — nothing is invented on purpose.
    • Remembers what you tell it (“My sister is called Anna”) — and truly forgets on request (“Forget my sister”).
    • Small talk, jokes, explanations (“Tell me about volcanoes”), calculations and unit conversion.
    • Runs entirely on your computer. Same question, same answer.

    Limits: English only for now, no internet, weaker at long creative writing. When unsure, ENGRAMM says so.
